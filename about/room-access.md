# Access to Computer Labs (CIP Pools)
[**Room 3238**](https://nav.tum.de/en/room/0502.03.238) is equipped with a LEGIC card reader to read your StudentCard. The registration process works as follows:  
1. Open the [**Link**](https://zope.ei.tum.de/login_form?came_from=https%3A//zope.ei.tum.de/IO/gate/legic-entry&retry=&disable_cookie_login__=1) in your browser.
2. Log in with your TUM email address (@mytum.de or @tum.de) and the password of your TUMonline account. To proceed, click "Log in". If you do not know your email address, use your TUMonline account name and append "@mytum.de" .
3. If you are not on the "Entry of the ID card number of YourName" page, click on "Registrieren der Nummer der StudentCard".
4. Now, the site requests the number of your StudentCard. This is the 5-to-8 digit (usually 6 digits) ID number (without a letter) on the front right side of your StudentCard (not your Matrikel-Nr.). Type in the ID-Number and confirm with *save*. Access to the room is instantly activated.

You can now enter other computer labs (CIP Pools) with your StudentCard, too, such as Room 3209 and Room N0199.

To log in to a TUM PC in a computer lab, use your TUM credentials:
> Username/Benutzername: Your TUMonline Username  
> Password/Kennwort: Your TUMonline Password
